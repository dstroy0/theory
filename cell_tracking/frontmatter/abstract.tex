\chapter*{Abstract}

\textbf{Nothing in this book has been measured yet.} It states a design, the reasons for it, and the
rows already in this work that predict where it fails. Every number below belongs to a different
subject and is quoted to bound this one. The book exists before the run so that the predictions are
on the page before the answer is, which is the arrangement three results in \texttt{theory/workbook}
did not have and were corrected for afterwards.

The subject is the Cell Tracking Challenge: published time-lapse microscopy, published segmentation,
and published ground truth linking every cell to itself across frames.

\textbf{Why this subject and not another.} The permutation null measure carries most of the findings
in this work and has no positive control from outside it. The workbook calls that the largest single
gap here. The shift agreement detector has three such controls, all published crystal cell edges, and
the two instruments are not interchangeable as evidence. A tracking benchmark ships an answer that
existed before this instrument was pointed at it and was not supplied by it, which is what a positive
control is. If the measure reads these sequences, it has its first external answer. If it does not,
that is a reading this work has never been able to take.

\textbf{What the existing rows predict.} They predict difficulty, and they are specific about the
cause. Proteins are the nearest domain already attempted and the construction found nothing there
twice. The first failure was the matcher: coordinates were voxelised at two angstroms and
displacements were required to agree exactly, and two occurrences of one motif never land on
identical offsets, because nature does not supply exact repeats. The second was the background: the
null shuffled values among fixed positions, which preserves every geometric constraint a fold obeys,
so all of the structure sat in both arms and cancelled. A third row, the dimension count, was
predicted to return three on every deposited structure and returned it on three of ten, with nearly
every score negative, because a folded chain is a thin object in a mostly empty box and the method
needs anisotropy spread through the set. A field of cells is a thin object in a mostly empty box.

\textbf{What differs here, and it is the reason to run it at all.} A domain that states its own rules
hands over the constant, and this one states them. A cell does not move arbitrarily between frames:
displacement is bounded, volume is roughly conserved, and a division puts two daughters of about half
the volume adjacent to where the parent was. That is the same kind of object as valence, which is why
the protein backbone walked as it was assembled returns chemistry's bond lengths to within 0.02
angstroms on five structures while the same molecules read as a scatter returned nothing. The
representation that keeps the constraint is the one that reads.

\textbf{The instrument with the external controls is the one pointed here.} Quantisation does not
destroy a sub-sample quantity, it moves it into a ratio: a period that is not a whole number of
voxels makes successive tiles land alternately on the two straddling lags, and the share of agreement
at the upper one carries the fraction. Read that way a published cell edge came back to 0.0006
angstroms against a voxel of 0.25, four hundred times finer than the slice. A cell's displacement
between two frames is not a whole number of pixels either, and the same relation should carry the
same sub-pixel remainder. That is the first prediction this book makes and it is testable on one
sequence.

\textbf{What is claimed and what is not.} Soundness is claimed and needs no data: any subset of a
pattern's points is a necessary condition, so a filter built from a subset loses no true linkage,
whatever selects it and whatever the dimension. Yield is not claimed. A universal claim about
applicability is deductive and holds; a universal claim about yield is refuted by rows already in
this work, where a memoryless corpus returns 1.00 and the digits of the square root of two return
1.00 and both are correct. An exact instrument reports exactly, including reporting nothing.

\textbf{One boundary is worth stating before anyone reads a number out of this.} The construction
measures a residue in an object. It does not reconstruct a generator and does not invoke Laplace's
demon, and an engine that infers a complete state from its laws is a separate measurement. Tracking
asks which cell is which, which is the member and not the family, and this work already measures that
split: one number identifies a family, the whole set is needed for a member, and nothing was found in
between. A cheap invariant rejects and never reconstructs. What follows is therefore a candidate
generator with a proof behind it and a scorer with none, run against an answer key, to find out which
of those two halves the benchmark is willing to pay for.
