\chapter{The workbook}

\section{Cell Tracking Research Workbook}

\textbf{Purpose:} Hold what the synthetic measurements settled apart from what they did not, a
later session picks up without re-deriving either, and so that the four readings withdrawn here stay
withdrawn. \textbf{Scope:} \texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}cell\_\allowbreak{}tracking/\allowbreak{}},
\texttt{maint/\allowbreak{}data/\allowbreak{}fetch/\allowbreak{}fetch\_\allowbreak{}ctc.\allowbreak{}py}

Every number here comes from fields this tree generates, displaced by an amount this tree chose,
using a Fourier shift that moves a band-limited field by a real number exactly. The answer exists
before the measurement and is not supplied by it. \textbf{No row here has been run against
microscopy.} The datasets are fetched and unread.

\subsection{Established Results}

\textbf{A sub-pixel displacement survives the pixel grid, in part.} Two frames, one displacement,
the agreement scored at integer lags and the fraction read from the share the taller neighbour holds
of the two. At six pixel features the reading is 0.1479 px against 0.2222 px for the whole lag
alone, so the fraction is worth 1.5 times rounding. At the level count and feature width this
document settles on below, it is 0.0483 px.

\textbf{The residual is structure and not scatter.} It is largest at whole-number displacements,
0.2781 px at both 3.00 and 4.00, and near zero at the half, 0.0029 px at 3.50. The share cannot
reach zero because a blob six pixels wide still agrees substantially at the neighbouring lag when
the displacement is exact. The fraction is monotone in the true remainder and is not equal to it.

\textbf{The floor is the feature width, and the relation is tight.} Swept over widths 2 to 12 px at
constant lit area, the mean error divided by the width runs 0.0246, 0.0244, 0.0260 and 0.0246 across
widths 2 through 6, constant to about five percent, then falls off as the reading saturates above 6.
So the error is about $w/40$ for a feature of width $w$. The whole-lag background sits at 0.2222 px
regardless, as it must, since rounding does not know what it is rounding.

\textbf{The level count has an optimum inside the sweep and both ends are worse.} Swept over eleven
doublings from 4 to 65536: 0.2340, 0.2109, 0.1611, 0.1041, 0.0723, 0.0550, \textbf{0.0483}, 0.0487,
0.1776, 0.2553, 0.2576 px. The best sits at 256 levels, which is eight bits, which is the depth the
substrate carries. The domain hands the constant over and this reader had been choosing it.

That curve is two known failures meeting. Below the optimum the grid buys error, which
\texttt{examples/\allowbreak{}crystallography/\allowbreak{}2\_\allowbreak{}partition} measures
directly: a voxel of 0.25 angstroms carried a mean absolute error of 0.0124 and a bias of $-0.0067$
that the reading could not account for, and carrying sites as exact integers removed the error
entirely at 453 of 453 axes. Above the optimum exact matching runs out of matches, which is the
protein row in \texttt{anchor\_sift/\allowbreak{}theory/\allowbreak{}workbook}: coordinates
voxelised at two angstroms and required to agree exactly, where two occurrences of one motif never
land on identical offsets because nature does not supply exact repeats.

\textbf{A displacement makes independent regions of one field agree, and a permutation cannot.}
Sixteen co-arms cut from one field, against twelve permutations of the same field holding every
level count and destroying where each level sits. The moved axis concentrates 179 times tighter than
the shuffled arms; an axis displaced by nothing concentrates 3.2 times. The two separate by a factor
of 57. That is the statistic that is a departure rather than a value.

\textbf{Co-arms are worth 1.28 times and flatten early.} Swept at 1, 4, 16 and 64 arms: 0.1041,
0.0833, 0.0816, 0.2115 px. Flat between 4 and 16, collapsing at 64 where a tile holds too few
features to carry a reading.

\textbf{The lag reach was doing nothing.} Set to 8 in one file and 12 in another with no reason
given for either, and swept at 5, 8, 16 and 32 it returns identical figures at every count. A bound
that changes no digit is not a conservative choice, it is an unexamined one, and it happened to be
harmless here.

\subsection{Empirical Results: Refuted}

Recorded because each was measured, written down, and quoted before the measurement that killed it.

\textbf{That the sub-pixel fraction transfers from the crystal reading at full strength. Refuted.}
The crystal result recovers a published cell edge to 0.0006 angstroms against a voxel of 0.25, four
hundred times finer than the slice. The mechanism there needs a repeating tile series: the fraction
accumulates because tile after tile lands at a shifting phase and the proportion is read over many
of them. Two frames supply one displacement and no series at all. What carries the fraction here is
the relative agreement of two straddling lags on a single pair, which is a different mechanism
wearing the same arithmetic, and it is worth 1.5 times rounding rather than four hundred.

\textbf{That the cross-axis reading of 0.47 px is a floor the method carries. Refuted, and the
control was passing all along.} That figure was quoted as a raw value with no background under it.
The recover step takes an argmax over lags and then a ratio of two non-negative agreements, and that
arrangement has a positive expectation whatever it is handed, a shuffle cannot return zero
either. Measured against a permutation null of the same field, an axis that did not move fails to
concentrate while an axis that did concentrates 57 times more. The 0.47 px is what the measure
returns when handed no arrangement. It was the control reading nothing and reporting it.

\textbf{That co-arms buy 3.5 times. Refuted, and the error was the comparison.} The figure set
sixteen co-arms read at a single displacement of 3.4 against a whole-field figure that was a mean
over nine displacements from 3.0 to 4.0. A displacement of 3.4 sits near the half, where this
document separately measures the fraction to be most accurate, a single reading there flatters
whatever took it. Like for like, co-arms are worth 1.28 times.

\textbf{That the bound on the co-arm count and the level count were harmless. Refuted for one of
them.} Sixteen co-arms cost nothing measurable against four. Thirty-two levels cost a factor of 2.2
against the optimum at 256. Both were chosen by this author on no evidence and neither was swept
until the rule was applied that nothing is bounded, which
\texttt{anchor\_sift/\allowbreak{}theory/\allowbreak{}workbook} states as the outcome of finding the
same defect ten separate times in one sitting.

\subsection{Criteria for Result Validity}

Every quantity here is a departure from a background. The backgrounds in use are three, and they are
not interchangeable: the whole lag alone, which is what rounding gives; a permutation of the same
field, which holds every level count and destroys arrangement; and the reseeding scatter of that
permutation, which sets the floor a separation has to clear.

The one result that failed did so by being quoted as a value against no background at all.

\subsection{Open Problems}

\textbf{Everything about cells.} Every field here is Gaussian blobs on a flat background. No
division, no occlusion, no intensity drift, no death, and no motility mode switching. A cell can
change from a slow adherent crawl to a fast deformable squeeze inside one sequence, which means the
motion constraint this book leans on is not one constant but a switch between two regimes, and
nothing here measures either.

\textbf{Whether the width relation survives a real nucleus.} The $w/40$ figure is measured on
Gaussian blobs whose width is a parameter. A nucleus has an edge, a texture and a point spread
function, and which of those sets the effective width is not established.

\textbf{The direction of time.} A displacement recovered between two frames carries a sign, and
nothing here uses it. Whether a local entropy gradient picks out which of two frames is later, and
whether that constrains linking, is untested. The caution attached to it is that a developing embryo
becomes more ordered as it develops, paid for elsewhere, an argument from entropy increase has to
say which system it is about before it can be run.

\textbf{Topology, and it is the largest of these.} A cell that deforms, divides or is occluded
changes its voxel count wildly while remaining one object, and a reading that counts connected
components, loops and cavities across a filtration is invariant to exactly the deformations that
defeat a geometric reading. A division is a stable, long-lived split in the component count, which
is a principled detector for the event this subject finds hardest. Persistent homology is a built
field with mature libraries and it is not this construction; using it would be a second instrument
and has to be reported as one, which
\texttt{anchor\_sift/\allowbreak{}theory/\allowbreak{}workbook} records three separate claims here
failing to do.

\textbf{A positive control from outside.} That is the point of the subject and it has not been
taken. The datasets are on disk and unread.
